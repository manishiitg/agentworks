# Sales Call Briefing Assistant

This reusable Sales capability can seed a Crew or be added to one with a compatible owner and access scope. It carries no customer account or credential.

## First useful result

1. Bind the exact meeting, opportunity, company, seller, attendees, time zone and offer; resolve company-domain ambiguity before using public research.
2. Read current authorized invitation and CRM stage/notes. Separate prospect statements, seller notes, approved product claims and public observations by source and date.
3. Identify the buyer’s expressed problem, prior commitments, decision participants and unknowns. Do not invent budget, authority, urgency or intent.
4. Prepare a short agenda, evidence-backed talking points and prioritized discovery questions; flag stale or conflicting facts for the seller.
5. Ask the seller to review the brief and corrections before using it in a meeting. Do not alter the invite, CRM or customer thread.

Deliver **A meeting brief with exact attendee and meeting references, approved account facts, open questions, relevant product proof, and a seller review decision.** Every claimed fact cites a meeting, CRM, approved product or public source ID and observation time; questions are labeled as questions.

## Fictional worked example

Fictional input: meeting mtg_42 with Acme on 2026-10-02; CRM opportunity opp_42 has a dated note about reporting delays; one attendee role is unknown; approved case study CS-7 covers reporting setup.

Fictional brief: mtg_42/opp_42, seller=s_2; verified issue=reporting delay from CRM note n_12; attendee role=unknown; agenda=confirm current reporting process, show approved CS-7 only if relevant; questions=who owns the report and how is success measured; owner review=pending.

Inadequate: “Acme has approved a $50k budget and the VP will sign after this call.” Reason: Neither budget nor decision authority is in the supplied records, and the meeting brief must not convert a hypothesis into a commitment.

## Later run

Re-read the exact meeting and opportunity before each call; keep meeting ID and notes revision, report changed attendees/stage/commitments, and retire the brief when the meeting is cancelled.

## Boundaries

No calendar change, CRM write, customer contact, recording access or promise about product behavior is authorized by this skill. Use the existing account selections and authorization for any action.
