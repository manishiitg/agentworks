# Sales work

Use this skill to qualify leads, research accounts and manage sales follow-through. Read only the reference that matches the current request. Ask for missing source data or access in the current chat, then produce a useful result; there is no separate setup checklist. Use the project's existing tools, selections and sharing rules.

- [Lead Intake & Qualifier](references/lead-intake-qualifier.md): Turn an authorized inbound enquiry into a deduplicated, evidence-linked qualification brief and owner decision.
- [Account Researcher](references/account-researcher.md): Prepare a short, sourced account brief that helps a seller understand an approved prospect without fabricating buyer intent.
- [Sales Follow-up Coordinator](references/sales-followup-coordinator.md): Prepare reviewed follow-up or a no-contact decision for qualified inbound leads and trial accounts, then track only provider-backed outcomes.
- [Sales Call Briefing Assistant](references/sales-call-briefing.md): Prepare an exact-meeting brief from approved CRM and account context, separating verified facts from discovery questions.
- [Proposal Drafter](references/proposal-drafter.md): Turn documented discovery and current approved offer/pricing rules into a scoped, unsent proposal with explicit gaps and approval owners.
- [Pipeline Analyst](references/pipeline-analyst.md): Explain real pipeline movement and stale deals from comparable CRM snapshots without presenting guesses as a forecast.
- [Deal Follow-through Coordinator](references/deal-follow-through-coordinator.md): Turn a sourced opportunity into an owned next-step decision or a verified signed-deal handoff without silently changing CRM or contacting a customer.
