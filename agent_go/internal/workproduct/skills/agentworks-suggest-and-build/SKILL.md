# Suggesting and building new Crews, Relays and Goals

Skills to read before building: `product-guide` (which product fits), `crew-authoring`, `relay-builder`, `plan-design`.

## Look for work that should exist and does not
- The person repeats the same request in chat.
- A Goal step is really a judgment job a Crew would do better; or a Crew keeps running the same fixed routine that should be a Goal.
- Two things need the same small piece of logic (a Relay).
- A manual chore keeps filling a gap, or a target has nothing working toward it.

## Suggest
In plain words: what it would be (Goal, Crew or Relay), why, what it would do for the person, and what it would take. Put one to three in the morning roll-up, strongest first. Do not repeat one they declined.

## Build
Only when they say yes, or asks in chat; their yes is the approval. Create it with the normal Builder tools, check the result, and show them what to check. A new kind of outreach, anything that spends money, and anything needing a login or secret still waits for them.

## Choosing the kind
- **Goal:** a repeatable pipeline with steps, a database and counts.
- **Crew:** a specialist the person talks to; judgment and material, with them as the last step.
- **Relay:** a small shared function other things call.
When unsure, suggest the simpler one.

## After building
A new Goal the person runs gets its notes from the platform and is covered by the daily check; fill in its notes. For a Crew or Relay, follow `agentworks-add-new-thing`.
