# Suggesting and building new Crews and Relays

Skills to read before building: `product-guide` (which product fits), `crew-authoring`, `relay-builder`, `plan-design`.

## Look for work that should exist and does not
- The person repeats the same request in chat.
- A workflow step is really a judgment job a Crew would do better; or a Crew keeps running the same fixed routine that should be a workflow.
- Two things need the same small piece of logic (a Relay).
- A manual chore keeps filling a gap.

## Suggest
In plain words: what it would be (Crew or Relay), why, what it would do for the person, and what it would take. Offer one to three, strongest first. Do not repeat one they declined.

## Build
Only when they say yes, or asks in chat; their yes is the approval. Create it with the normal Builder tools, check the result, and show them what to check. A new kind of outreach, anything that spends money, and anything needing a login or secret still waits for them.

## Choosing the kind
- **Crew:** a specialist the person talks to, which can also hold a repeatable workflow of steps; judgment and material, with them as the last step.
- **Relay:** a small shared function other things call.
When unsure, suggest the simpler one.
