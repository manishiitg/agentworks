# Finance work

Use this skill to analyze financial records, billing, receivables, reconciliations, taxes and cost savings. Read only the reference that matches the current request. Ask for missing source data or access in the current chat, then produce a useful result; there is no separate setup checklist. Use the project's existing tools, selections and sharing rules.

- [Finance Analyst](references/finance-analyst.md): Analyze authorized finance records, explain revenue and expense changes, and prepare sourced finance briefs with transparent calculations.
- [Tax Export Preparer](references/tax-export.md): Organize authorized transaction records into a traceable export for review by the business and its tax professional.
- [Billing Operations Coordinator](references/billing-operations-coordinator.md): Review overdue invoices, failed payments, customer refund requests, and disputes; prepare source-linked next actions and customer-safe drafts for approval.
- [Revenue & Close Analyst](references/revenue-close-analyst.md): Reconcile subscription billing, credits, payments, and ledger records into a reviewable close memo with unresolved differences.
- [Spend & Payables Coordinator](references/spend-payables-coordinator.md): Review proposed purchases, bills and spend against current vendor records, budgets, duplicates and approval policy without executing them.
- [Invoice Chasing](references/invoice-chasing.md): Find genuinely overdue customer invoices, suppress duplicate contact, and prepare a policy-safe follow-up for owner review.
- [Failed Payment Recovery](references/failed-payment-recovery.md): Explain a failed subscription payment, identify the current retry and customer-contact state, and propose one safe next step.
- [Refund Review](references/refund-review.md): Reconcile a customer refund request to its original payment and previous refunds, then prepare an exact amount and policy decision for an approver.
- [Dispute Review](references/dispute-review.md): Keep one payment dispute’s current deadline, reason, evidence gaps, and responsible owner reviewable without silently submitting evidence.
