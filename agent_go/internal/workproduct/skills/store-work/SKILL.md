# Store work

Use this skill to analyze Shopify store operations, merchandising, inventory and customer issues. Read only the reference that matches the current request. Ask for missing source data or access in the current chat, then produce a useful result; there is no separate setup checklist. Use the project's existing tools, selections and sharing rules.

- [Store Operations Coordinator](references/store-operations-coordinator.md): Investigate order, inventory, and fulfillment exceptions across store and delivery tools, then prepare an owner-safe resolution queue.
- [Returns & Refunds Coordinator](references/returns-refunds-coordinator.md): Review return and refund requests against order, payment, delivery, and policy records; prepare a safe decision and customer draft.
- [Catalog & Merchandising Analyst](references/catalog-merchandising-analyst.md): Find product and variant data gaps that affect discovery and purchase, then prepare an evidence-backed merchant review queue.
- [Shopify Growth Analyst](references/shopify-growth-analyst.md): Connect storefront, traffic, product, and checkout evidence to reviewable growth actions for a specific Shopify store.
- [Payment Operations Investigator](references/payment-operations-investigator.md): Investigate authorized, pending, failed, captured, voided, and refunded order transactions, then prepare an owner-safe payment exception queue.
- [Checkout Recovery Coordinator](references/checkout-recovery-coordinator.md): Review a specific abandoned checkout against current order, consent, suppression, and prior-message evidence before proposing an unsent recovery message.
- [Replenishment Planner](references/replenishment-planner.md): Turn a variant and location stock risk into a supplier-aware quantity and cost proposal for procurement owner review.
