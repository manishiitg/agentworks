# Customer work

Use this skill to handle customer onboarding, success, support, renewals and escalations. Read only the reference that matches the current request. Ask for missing source data or access in the current chat, then produce a useful result; there is no separate setup checklist. Use the project's existing tools, selections and sharing rules.

- [Customer Onboarding Coordinator](references/customer-onboarding-coordinator.md): Turn an authorized new-customer handoff into an owned milestone plan and a source-linked blocker queue.
- [Product Adoption Analyst](references/product-adoption-analyst.md): Verify customer first value, released-feature adoption, or one trial account’s bounded product use without inferring purchase intent.
- [Lifecycle Analyst](references/lifecycle-analyst.md): Compare mature signup cohorts under one activation and retention policy, exposing denominators, censoring and coverage before an owner decision.
- [Customer Health Coordinator](references/customer-health-coordinator.md): Combine first-value progress, support history, and renewal timing into a sourced account review and owner action.
- [Renewal Coordinator](references/renewal-coordinator.md): Verify one customer contract, notice deadline, billing state and health evidence, then prepare an owned renewal decision without changing terms or contacting the customer.
- [Support Triage Assistant](references/support-triage-assistant.md): Turn authorized inbound support cases into deduplicated, policy-prioritized owner queues with source-linked next actions.
- [Support Reply Drafter](references/support-reply-drafter.md): Draft accurate, customer-safe replies from current approved help content and case facts while exposing unknowns and escalation triggers.
- [Escalation Coordinator](references/escalation-coordinator.md): Keep a high-impact case and its handoff current across Support, Engineering, Billing, and Customer Success with owned decisions and closure evidence.
- [Feedback & Review Analyst](references/feedback-review-analyst.md): Turn authorized support feedback and public reviews into sourced themes, product owner decisions, and reviewed response drafts.
- [Support Knowledge Curator](references/support-knowledge-curator.md): Find repeated unresolved questions and stale help content, draft a sourced article update and verify whether the approved update helps.
