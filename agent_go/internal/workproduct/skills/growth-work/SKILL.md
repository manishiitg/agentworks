# Growth work

Use this skill to research website growth, marketing, campaigns and go-to-market decisions. Read only the reference that matches the current request. Ask for missing source data or access in the current chat, then produce a useful result; there is no separate setup checklist. Use the project's existing tools, selections and sharing rules.

- [Website Growth Starter](references/website-growth-starter.md): Audit the business website, find evidence-backed opportunities for relevant traffic, and guide a measurable 30-day growth plan.
- [SEO Analyst](references/seo-analyst.md): Find and prioritize observable crawl, indexability, metadata, internal-link, and page-experience issues.
- [Search Opportunity Mapper](references/search-opportunity-mapper.md): Map target buyers’ questions and search intent to existing pages and evidence-backed content gaps.
- [Content Brief Writer](references/content-brief-writer.md): Turn an approved search or buyer opportunity into a useful page brief with claims, sources, internal links, and a success signal.
- [Content Page Builder](references/content-page-builder.md): Draft a useful, source-grounded website page from an approved brief without publishing it automatically.
- [Website Publishing Coordinator](references/website-publishing-coordinator.md): Carry an approved page draft through a separately authorized publication route and verify the exact live page before recording a shipped change.
- [Search Console Optimizer](references/search-console-optimizer.md): Find page/query opportunities in authorized Search Console data and propose bounded page improvements.
- [Traffic & Engagement Analyst](references/traffic-engagement-analyst.md): Explain relevant visitor, landing-page, and conversion changes from authorized analytics data.
- [AI Visibility Analyst](references/ai-visibility-analyst.md): Test approved buyer questions across selected answer engines and report brand/citation gaps with repeatable evidence.
- [Landing Page Optimizer](references/landing-page-optimizer.md): Inspect one visitor journey and propose a bounded page revision or test grounded in evidence.
- [Content Distribution Coordinator](references/content-distribution-coordinator.md): Find relevant channels and prepare reviewable distribution and outreach drafts for a published asset.
- [Competitor Intelligence Analyst](references/competitor-intelligence-analyst.md): Track a bounded competitor set for sourced changes that matter to a named offer, buyer, and decision owner.
- [Campaign Performance Analyst](references/campaign-performance-analyst.md): Explain a bounded campaign’s spend, delivery, qualified response, and conversion changes from source records with comparable definitions.
- [Funnel Analyst](references/funnel-analyst.md): Reconcile an authorized signup-to-paid cohort across event and billing sources, then identify a bounded drop-off for owner review.
- [Growth Experiment Planner](references/growth-experiment-planner.md): Turn one evidence-backed growth question into a bounded experiment with a decision rule, owner, safety limit, and measurement plan.
- [Experiment Run Coordinator](references/experiment-run-coordinator.md): Carry one approved experiment design through exact launch-state checks, provider receipts and rollback ownership without treating a proposal as permission.
- [Growth Outcome Analyst](references/growth-outcome-analyst.md): Reconcile an actual launched experiment with its frozen metric, assignment and observation window, then report measured or inconclusive outcomes for owner review.
- [GTM Strategy Analyst](references/gtm-strategy-analyst.md): Turn the offer, buyer evidence, market, and constraints into a sourced launch brief with a testable pipeline goal.
- [Launch Coordinator](references/launch-coordinator.md): Keep approved launch assets, channel actions, owners, dates, dependencies, and observed lead signals in one source-linked ledger.
- [Revenue Operations Analyst](references/revenue-operations-analyst.md): Reconcile campaign, form, lead, account and opportunity records under one stage and attribution policy for a shared GTM owner decision.
- [Sales Enablement Coordinator](references/sales-enablement-coordinator.md): Prepare current, buyer-specific seller guidance from approved product claims, customer proof and objections without inventing promises.
- [Pricing & Packaging Analyst](references/pricing-packaging-analyst.md): Compare current plan entitlements, buyer evidence, unit economics and sales exceptions to prepare a reviewed package or price decision.
