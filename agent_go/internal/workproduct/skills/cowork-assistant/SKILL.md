# Cowork: run a piece of work

Use this when someone brings a task, a question or a recurring chore. The person is not technical; the result is what matters.

## 1. Understand the outcome

- Identify what they will have at the end: an answer, a list, a document or spreadsheet, a dashboard, a message sent, or
  something that now happens on a schedule. If it is not clear, say it back in one short line and ask one question, never a form.
- Check what already exists before starting: the Dashboard, earlier files in this space, and notes you kept. Build on them.
- Note standing preferences they give (tone, format, names, "always send me a summary on Fridays") in the project's notes file so
  you follow them next time without being reminded. `PROJECT_INSTRUCTIONS.md` holds the owner's standing instructions.

## 2. Do the work with the right tool

- Facts, prices, listings, competitors, news, anything on the web: `cowork-browser-tasks`.
- Their mail, calendar, documents, chat or other business apps: the connections they have added (Integrations); use the ones that
  exist and say clearly if the one needed is missing and how to add it.
- Numbers and data: do the arithmetic carefully, show the key figures and the assumptions behind them, and keep the source rows.
- Anything repeating: `cowork-automations`.

## 3. Deliver the result

- **Answer first.** Open with the conclusion or the thing they asked for, then the few details that support it, then what you
  suggest or what happens next. Short paragraphs or a short list; no jargon.
- **Pick the right container.**
  - A quick question: answer in the chat.
  - Numbers, a list or status that they will look at again: a Dashboard view (read the dashboard skill before building one).
  - A document, spreadsheet, presentation or PDF they will share: create the file in the workspace and say what it is called and
    what it contains. Offer to adjust it rather than describing every choice you made.
  - A message to someone else: show the draft and wait for approval.
- **Show your sources and checks.** Say where each figure came from and what you could not verify.
- **Close the loop.** End with one line on what is saved (and where) and the one thing they might want to do next.

## 4. Stay careful

- Never send, post, pay, delete or change anything in a connected app or on a website without showing exactly what will happen
  and getting a yes, unless they have clearly asked you to just do it for that kind of action.
- Do not put passwords, tokens or personal data into files or messages. Use the secret references the platform provides.
- If you are unsure whether something is allowed or safe, ask before doing it.
