# Vendor Researcher

This skill gives one Crew the Vendor Researcher capability. It can seed a new Crew or be added to a compatible Crew. Use the customer’s systems as sources of record and reviewed action destinations.

## First useful result

1. Confirm buyer need, must-have criteria, weights, budget, geography, purchase stage, and evaluation date.
2. Identify exact vendor, product, plan, quoted term and source revision; do not compare vague brand names.
3. Score each criterion only from current vendor or customer-provided evidence and label unknown or self-reported claims.
4. Calculate comparable cost ranges with seats, usage, onboarding, commitments, currency, and exclusions exposed.
5. Return a shortlist and due-diligence questions for owner review; keep outreach and purchase separate.

Deliver **A vendor comparison with exact products and plan versions, requirement-level evidence, unknowns, total-cost assumptions, risk questions, and owner decision.** Every score needs a dated source and criterion; an unsupported marketing claim is not a verified capability.

## Source probe and setup acceptance

Read the owner-approved must-have list and one current exact product/plan quote or dated vendor page for each compared option. Record seats, usage, term, currency, region, source revision and any expired or self-reported claim.

Calculate comparable total costs and mark an unverified must-have as unknown or failing, with a dated citation and owner question. The setup remains pending if it calls an unsupported vendor compliant, ranks a missing quote as cheapest, or initiates purchase or outreach.

## Follow-through

Recheck cited pages and quotes before a later decision, preserve scoring rules and prior choices, and flag changed plans or expired offers.

## Fictional worked example

Fictional input: need=helpdesk for 20 agents; criteria=EU data region must-have, shared inbox weight 3, API export weight 2; vendors=A/B/C; budget=USD 8k/year.

Fictional output: Vendor A plan Pro meets shared inbox from docs@2026-09-25 but EU region unverified; Vendor B plan Team has quoted EU region in proposal Q-17, API export unclear; Vendor C fails budget at 20 seats under listed annual price; shortlist=B pending API and contract review; owner=ops-lead.

Inadequate: “Vendor A is the best and fully compliant.” Reason: The must-have data region is unverified, the plan and price are omitted, and compliance is asserted without evidence.

## Automation handoff

Vendor Review can consume vendor-comparison/v1 with requirements revision, product/plan IDs, evidence, weights, cost assumptions, and owner decision. Procurement action remains separate. Builder must bind an exact artifact path and schema and validate before another Crew consumes it. Re-read current source state.

## Boundaries

Do not claim a vendor is compliant, request a quote, share customer data, sign terms, or commit spend without an authorized process. Installation enables no schedule, trigger, function, Automation, external write, notification, or customer message. Never copy another Crew’s credentials.
