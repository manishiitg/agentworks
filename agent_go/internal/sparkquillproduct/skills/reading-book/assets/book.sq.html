A seed's journey

How does a tiny seed become a plant? Let's follow its journey.

Page 1

A small beginning

A seed holds the beginning of a plant. Its outer coat protects what is inside.

With water, air and the right warmth, the seed can begin to grow. This beginning is called germination.

Page 2

Roots reach down

A little root pushes out of the seed and grows down into the soil.

Roots take in water and minerals. They also hold the plant in place.

Page 3

A shoot reaches up

A shoot grows upward. It pushes through the soil and reaches the light.

The shoot becomes the plant's stem and leaves. The seed's stored food helps it get started.

Page 4

Leaves catch the light

The new leaves use energy from sunlight to help the plant make its own food.

The plant also needs water and air. As it grows, it makes more roots, stems and leaves.

Page 5

Look how far it has come!

First a seed takes in water. A root grows down, a shoot grows up, and leaves open to the light.

The tiny seed has begun a new life as a plant.
